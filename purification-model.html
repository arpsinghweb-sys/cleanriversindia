💧 Water Purification Model

From Polluted Water to Safer Water

Understanding the scientific stages of water treatment.

Our Proposed Treatment Process

Water treatment requires different stages depending on the type and level of contamination.

1

Screening

Removes large waste such as plastic, leaves, cloth and other solid materials.

2

Grit Removal

Removes sand, gravel and other heavy particles from incoming wastewater.

3

Coagulation & Flocculation

Suitable chemicals help tiny suspended particles join together into larger flocs.

4

Sedimentation

Heavier flocs settle at the bottom and can be removed as sludge.

5

Biological Treatment

Microorganisms break down biodegradable organic pollutants under controlled conditions.

6

Filtration

Sand, membrane or other suitable filters remove remaining suspended particles.

7

Advanced Treatment

Activated carbon and other technologies can remove selected chemicals and contaminants.

8

Disinfection

Suitable disinfection methods such as UV, ozone or chlorine reduce harmful microorganisms.

9

Laboratory Testing

Water is tested against applicable quality standards before its intended use.

Important: Is Treated Water Safe to Drink?

Not necessarily. Treated wastewater or river water cannot automatically be considered drinking water. It requires appropriate advanced treatment, laboratory testing and compliance with drinking-water standards.

What Happens to the Removed Waste?

Screening waste and treatment sludge must be collected, treated and disposed of safely. Untreated sludge must never be dumped back into rivers.
