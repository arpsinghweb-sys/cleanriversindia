💧 Clean Rivers India

A Public Initiative for River Protection and Scientific Collaboration

1. Introduction

Clean Rivers India is a proposed public-interest initiative to encourage citizens, environmental experts, researchers and institutions to work together for cleaner rivers and healthier water ecosystems.

हमारा उद्देश्य केवल समस्या बताना नहीं, बल्कि सही जानकारी, वैज्ञानिक जाँच, जनभागीदारी और जिम्मेदार संस्थागत सहयोग के माध्यम से समाधान को बढ़ावा देना है।

2. Problems We Want to Address

  • Untreated domestic sewage entering rivers and drains.
  • Industrial wastewater and chemical contamination.
  • Plastic and solid-waste dumping.
  • Damage to aquatic life and river ecosystems.
  • Lack of accessible, verified local pollution information.
  • Low public awareness about water quality and safe reporting.

3. Our Proposed Approach

  • Encourage citizens to report pollution from safe locations.
  • Verify reports before publishing them as confirmed information.
  • Promote water-quality testing through qualified laboratories.
  • Invite researchers to study pollution sources and suitable treatment methods.
  • Share educational material in simple Indian languages.
  • Support coordination between citizens and relevant authorities.

4. Scientific Collaboration

We invite universities, environmental scientists, water-quality laboratories and engineering institutions to help evaluate pollution data and develop location-specific solutions.

Any purification or restoration proposal must be based on laboratory testing, applicable environmental standards, technical feasibility and safe waste management.

5. Public Participation

  • Students and teachers can support environmental education.
  • Local volunteers can share observations and verified information.
  • Researchers can help assess water quality and treatment options.
  • Organizations can explore awareness and restoration partnerships.

6. Support Requested

  • Technical guidance from water and environmental experts.
  • Advice on reliable water-quality testing and data verification.
  • Opportunities for academic and institutional collaboration.
  • Guidance on responsible reporting and public participation.
  • Evaluation of future pilot projects, subject to permissions and resources.

7. Our Commitment

We will aim to maintain transparency, protect personal information, verify public reports and avoid unsupported claims. We do not claim government approval, official partnership, funding or completed river-cleaning work at this stage.

8. Invitation to Collaborate

We welcome guidance and constructive participation from government departments, research institutions, universities, environmental organizations and concerned citizens.

Together, we can work towards cleaner rivers and a healthier future.
