🌱 Health & Environment

Healthy Rivers, Healthy People

Understand how river pollution affects our lives and nature.

1. Effects on Human Health

🦠 Waterborne Diseases

Contaminated water can spread diseases such as cholera, typhoid and diarrhoea.

☣️ Chemical Exposure

Some industrial chemicals and heavy metals can cause long-term health problems.

🧫 Microorganisms

Harmful bacteria, viruses and parasites can make unsafe water dangerous to consume.

2. Effects on Environment

🐟 Aquatic Life

Pollution and low oxygen levels can harm fish and other aquatic organisms.

🌳 Ecosystem Damage

Pollution can disturb the natural balance of river ecosystems.

🌾 Soil and Groundwater

Some pollutants can move into soil and groundwater, depending on local conditions.

3. How Does Water Become Unsafe?

Untreated sewage, industrial discharge, agricultural runoff and solid waste can introduce harmful contaminants into rivers. Water that looks clean is not necessarily safe to drink.

4. How Can We Protect Ourselves?

💧 Test Water

Use appropriate laboratory tests to check drinking-water quality.

🚰 Treat Water

Use a treatment method suitable for the specific contamination present.

🏥 Follow Health Advice

Follow local public-health guidance when water contamination is reported.

5. Our Responsibility

Do not throw plastic, garbage or chemicals into rivers. Report visible pollution to the appropriate authorities. Support scientific monitoring and responsible river restoration.
