Our Mission

Our mission is to bring citizens, volunteers, scientists and organizations together to protect and restore India's rivers.

Why Do Our Rivers Need Protection?

🏭 Industrial Waste

Untreated industrial discharge can pollute river water.

🏠 Sewage

Untreated domestic wastewater harms river ecosystems.

🗑️ Plastic Pollution

Plastic waste threatens aquatic life and water quality.
